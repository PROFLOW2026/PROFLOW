# Calendar four lenses (PM-011)

Dates appear through four product lenses (intentionally separate):

1. **Project milestones** — contract/milestone targets on the project record.
2. **Planning / Gantt** — planning work items and dependencies (`work_kind=project` only).
3. **Tasks calendar** — assignee due dates and task calendar views.
4. **Coordination events** — multi-party readiness meetings (Developer–GC track).

External Google/Outlook sync remains **deferred** (PM-012). Internal surfaces should deep-link across lenses but do not assume one unified calendar table.
